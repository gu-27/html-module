Your Own Site

Build a multi-page site on any topic you want, using everything from HTML-1 to HTML-4.

Unlock the full editor

You've earned it. On your repo's main page, press the . (period) key. A full code editor opens in your browser, with color-coding and error underlines.

  1. In the file list on the left, right-click assignment4 → Copy.
  2. Right-click an empty spot in the list → Paste. Rename the copy to assignment5.
  3. Click the Source Control icon (the branch icon on the left), type a message, and click Commit & Push.

Now change the files in assignment5 to fit your new idea: new file names (except index.html), new menu images and new content.

Making menu images: design small buttons in Canva or any image tool. Export as PNG, give them simple lowercase names, and upload them. Or skip images and style text links with CSS.

Requirements

  1. Depth: your site uses everything we covered: links, tables and external CSS.
  2. Friendly: it's easy to find links and to move back and forth between pages.
  3. Visual appeal: think about fonts (size and family), colors, images and overall balance.

What earns points

  • CSS: 2 new styles beyond the template earns half, 6 new styles earns full.2 pts
  • Tables: 3 properly formatted tables.1 pt
  • Links: 3 external links (email, other sites), 2 internal links (within a page), and no broken links. The menu bar doesn't count.1 pt
  • Images: 3 new images earns half, 6 new images that are sized and balanced earns full.3 pts
  • Home page: named index.html, so your link ends in /assignment5/, not a file name.1 pt
  • Consistent menu: linked on every page, with images, using your CSS.2 pts

Make it yours

  • Fill in the author comment at the top of every file with your name and GU username.
  • Fill in <meta name="author"> with your name.
  • Fill in the footer: Built by YOUR NAME · BMIS 235.
  • Make at least 3 commits with clear messages (not "Update index.html").
  • Your own topic, design and images. No two sites should look alike.

When you're stuck

  1. Say it in one sentence: "I expected ___, but I see ___."
  2. Pick one problem. Ignore the rest for now.
  3. Find the line. Which tag or file is it in?
  4. Search your one sentence (W3Schools, Google, YouTube).
  5. Change one thing, commit, check. Didn't work? Undo it and try the next idea.

"Nothing works" can't be searched. "My image shows a broken icon" can.
