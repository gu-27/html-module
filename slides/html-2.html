1 · Two tags that bring things in

<img>

A picture.

<img src="gervais.jpg">

<iframe>

A whole other web page, in a box.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/...">

You write the img. The other site writes the iframe and you paste it.

2 · Hosted vs borrowed

Hosted

A file in your repo. gervais.jpg

It stays put. Only you change it.

Borrowed

Comes from someone else's site every time your page loads. A linked image, a YouTube video, a map.

Quick to add. They're in charge.

3 · Why borrowed can break

They move the image
→
Broken icon on your page
They block embedding
→
"Video unavailable"

Not your code. Your page didn't change, theirs did. Companies check their pages for exactly this.

4 · YouTube embeds

  1. Under the video: Share → Embed.
  2. Copy the address inside src="...". It contains /embed/.
  3. Paste it into the iframe's src on your page.
youtube.com/watch?v=pq6WHJzOkno   ← watch link
youtube.com/embed/pq6WHJzOkno     ← embed link

5 · Business embeds

  • Google Map: the "Find us" box on a restaurant or clinic site.
  • Google Form: event sign-ups, customer surveys, job applications on a small business site.
  • Tableau Public dashboard: a city's budget page, a team's stats page, a nonprofit's annual report.

All three hand you an <iframe>. Same move every time: copy, paste, commit.

6 · Image links and jump links

<a href="#huberman"><img src="..."></a>   ← click

...

<a id="huberman"></a>                     ← land here

Wrap anything in <a> to make it clickable. #name jumps to the matching id on the same page.

7 · What is a script?

A tiny program inside the page. It makes the page do something.

<button onclick="toggleDark()">Dark mode on / off</button>
<script>
   // change these two colors
   var lightBackground = "lightgray";
   var darkBackground  = "#333333";
   ...
</script>

You only touch the two colors. The rest is the program.

When you're stuck

  1. Say it in one sentence: "I expected ___, but I see ___."
  2. Pick one problem.
  3. Find the line.
  4. Search your one sentence.
  5. Change one thing. Commit. Check.
